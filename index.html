About us

A studio made for children's books

CREATIViUS is an illustration studio made for children's books. We draw soft, colorful worlds that feel warm and welcoming to young readers.

We work closely with authors and publishers so every page matches the heart of the story.

Projects outside our signature style are welcome, but please contact us first so we can confirm.

How to order

  1. Reach out

  2. Share your brief

    Story, page count, book size, and deadline.

  3. Quote and down payment

    We send a quote. Work starts after the down payment.

  4. Sketches

    We send sketches for your feedback.

  5. Revisions

    We adjust the art based on your notes.

    Final coloring and delivery of your files.
